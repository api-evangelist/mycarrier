If you ship carpet, foam, film, textiles, or anything else that comes on a roll, you already know standard LTL pricing does not always tell the full story. A roll takes up more trailer space per pound than a typical palletized shipment, and most rate engines were not built with that shape in mind. That gap is exactly what MyCarrier's new Rolled Goods Rates feature is designed to close.
