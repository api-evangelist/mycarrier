In LTL (Less-than-Truckload) shipping, even the most efficient operations can be caught off guard by hidden costs. Accessorial charges—like liftgate fees, limited access deliveries, or cubic-capacity adjustments—often appear after a shipment is booked, creating surprises for shippers and added friction with carriers. These charges aren’t arbitrary; they stem from complex rules, location nuances, and shipment details that are easy to miss at the quoting stage.
