Freight theft is on the rise—and shippers are paying attention. From stolen freight to fraudulent pickups and shipment diversions, the risk landscape is evolving fast. For businesses shipping less-than-truckload (LTL), whose freight often passes through multiple terminals and hands, this creates a critical need for protection that goes beyond prevention.
