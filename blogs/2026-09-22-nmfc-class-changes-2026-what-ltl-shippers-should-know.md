NMFTA 's Docket 2026-2 is a proposal to change roughly 33 items in the National Motor Freight Classification (NMFC) system, the standard that assigns freight class based on a commodity's density, stowability, handling, and liability. It follows a July 2025 overhaul that already moved more than 2,000 commodities to density-based classification, and it could reshape freight class, and therefore rates, for shippers who ship any of the affected commodities.
