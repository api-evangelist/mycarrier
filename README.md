MyCarrier is a transportation management system (TMS) for less-than-truckload (LTL) and full-truckload freight, founded in 2018 by Michael Bookout and Chris Scheid. The platform lets shippers quote, book, dispatch, track, audit and pay freight from a single connected workspace using direct carrier rates rather than broker markups, and connects to more than a dozen national LTL carriers. MyCarrier publishes a public REST API on developer.mycarrier.io covering order creation and management, rate quoting and dispatch, routing-guide rules, shipping locations, shipment detail retrieval, and a FreightAudit / invoice surface for freight bill audit, disputes, notes and payments. It also publishes a webhook catalog for shipment and invoice lifecycle events.

**Areas:** Company · Logistics · Transportation · Freight · Shipping · LTL · Supply Chain · Transportation Management · Freight Audit · Invoicing
